Interpretation and limitations

Fixed initial configurations; bounded ML training; full evaluation. Four adjacent windows do not establish significance. Stratified subsets are separate, unweighted comparisons. Missing historical prices remain missing; future events/SNAP/prices are excluded. Historical price publication latency is unknown. Point forecasts are recursively fed back, while observed activity/price context stays frozen.

Pipeline wall times include cached and cold preparation. Grouped hierarchy results are leaf-series errors, not official M5 WRMSSE. Source sales can be stock constrained. Direct model transfer has not been demonstrated. Protected final holdouts remain untouched.
